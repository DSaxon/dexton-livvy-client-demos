About Apeeling LLC

Building The Future Of Upcycled Ingredients.

Apeeling LLC is an ingredient innovation company transforming citrus byproducts into high-value ingredients for industries around the world — sustainable, functional, and built for a circular economy.

Who We Are

An Ingredient Company, Reimagining What's Possible.

Apeeling LLC was founded on a simple but powerful idea: the materials others discard can become some of the most valuable ingredients of the future. We are an ingredient innovation company dedicated to transforming citrus byproducts into functional, sustainable ingredients for the food, beverage, personal care, wellness, and agricultural industries.

We are not in the business of selling orange peels. We are building a portfolio of upcycled ingredients — beginning with Orange Peel Granules™ — engineered to help manufacturers innovate while advancing a more circular economy.

Every ingredient we develop is designed to deliver real functional value while keeping resources in circulation and out of the waste stream.

What Drives Us

Three Principles. One Mission.

⚗️

Ingredient Innovation

We develop functional ingredients from materials that already exist — unlocking new value and new applications without creating new waste.

🌍

Sustainability

Circular Manufacturing
