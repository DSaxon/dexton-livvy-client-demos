Our First Ingredient

Orange Peel Granules™

Orange Peel Granules™ are premium upcycled citrus ingredients produced from carefully processed orange peels and developed for commercial applications across multiple industries.

Orange peel has long been valued for its natural aroma, flavor profile, fiber content, and botanical properties. Our granules give formulators a versatile, naturally derived ingredient that is both functional and environmentally responsible.

  • SourceUpcycled orange peel
  • FormGranulated citrus ingredient
  • AttributesNatural aroma & flavor, fiber content, botanical properties
  • ApplicationsFood, beverage, personal care, agriculture, wellness
