The Future of Ingredients

Starts With What Others Leave Behind.

At Apeeling LLC, we believe that some of the world's most valuable ingredients are hiding in plain sight. For generations, citrus byproducts have been overlooked, discarded, or underutilized — despite containing functional properties that can serve numerous industries.

Our company was founded to change that. By transforming citrus materials into premium ingredients, we are creating solutions that help manufacturers innovate, reduce waste, and participate in a more circular economy.

Today, our first ingredient is Orange Peel Granules™. Tomorrow, our ingredient portfolio will continue to expand as we unlock new opportunities in upcycled ingredient development.

Our First Ingredient

Orange Peel Granules™

Orange Peel Granules™ are premium upcycled citrus ingredients produced from carefully processed orange peels and developed for commercial applications across multiple industries.

Orange peel has long been valued for its natural aroma, flavor profile, fiber content, and botanical properties. As consumer demand continues to shift toward natural and sustainable ingredients, manufacturers are seeking innovative ways to incorporate citrus ingredients into their products.

Our Orange Peel Granules™ provide a versatile ingredient solution that allows companies to formulate with ingredients that are both functional and environmentally responsible.
