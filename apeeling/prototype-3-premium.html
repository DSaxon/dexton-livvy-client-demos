About Apeeling LLC

Nature-forward textiles. Global scale.

We are a full-service textile company built on a singular belief — the best fabrics come from nature. Apeeling LLC partners with brands, manufacturers, and distributors who demand quality without compromise and who believe the future of textiles is rooted in sustainable, natural ingredients.

We source from the earth,
with respect for it."

Our Commitment

Sustainability isn't a checkbox

We've built sustainability into the DNA of Apeeling — not as a marketing position, but as an operating principle that guides every sourcing, production, and logistics decision we make.

"The earth provides the ingredients. We are responsible for what we do with them."

Natural Sourcing First

We prioritize naturally grown and harvested raw materials, working exclusively with suppliers who meet our environmental sourcing standards.

Water-Conscious Processing

Our dyeing and finishing operations use closed-loop water systems and low-impact dyes to minimize consumption and chemical discharge.

End-of-Life Design Thinking

We design for circularity — helping clients select fibers and finishes that allow for recyclability, biodegradation, or material recovery.

Ethical Global Standards

We audit and hold every partner in our supply chain to rigorous social and environmental compliance standards — globally, consistently.
