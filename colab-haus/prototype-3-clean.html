Leadership

The person behind the work.

April N. Richardson, Esq.
Founder · Attorney · Operator

April N. Richardson, Esq.

Founder · Attorney · Operator

April operates at the intersection of business, law, and execution. With over two decades as a Business and Real Estate Attorney and more than 15 years as a Food Entrepreneur, she brings practical experience that extends beyond advisory and into the work itself.

Her background began in law enforcement as a White Collar Crimes Investigator and Prosecutor at the local and federal level — handling complex matters involving fraud, financial systems, and multi-party coordination. She has trained and worked alongside the Secret Service and FBI.

That foundation carried into her legal and business career. She founded The Private Practice Law Group, where she has advised hundreds of businesses ranging from early-stage companies to established organizations. In parallel, she built and operates multiple companies whose products are distributed through Starbucks, Nordstrom, Wegmans, Safeway, and QVC.
