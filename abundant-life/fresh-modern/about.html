Our story

Rooted in the community garden.

Abundant Life Market grew out of a simple belief: fresh, clean food should be within reach for everyone in the DMV.

A grower, a garden, and a neighborhood.

The market was started by a local grower and community builder who spent years working the soil in a DMV community garden. What started as sharing extra harvest with neighbors became a mission: make real, whole food easy to get for the people right down the road.

Today we bring that same spirit to a wider table — sourcing directly from sustainable family farms in Maryland and Pennsylvania, and carrying it fresh to doorsteps across the DMV.

What we stand for

Every product on the shelf has to clear the same bar.

Straight from the farm

We buy directly from growers and ranchers — no middleman marking up your food or blurring where it came from.

Pasture-raised & clean

Animals raised on pasture, soy-free where it counts, and produce grown organically. No shortcuts.

Picked at peak

Harvested the same week it ships, so what lands on your table still tastes like the field it came from.

For the community

Rooted in the DMV, built to make clean food accessible to the neighbors we serve.
