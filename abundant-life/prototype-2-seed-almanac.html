Our Story

Abundant Life Market is rooted in the community garden. Started by a local grower and community builder, it was born from a simple belief: fresh, clean food should be within reach for everyone in the DMV.

We source directly from sustainable farms in Maryland and Pennsylvania — pasture-raised animals, organic produce, and whole-food supplements, with no shortcuts and no middleman. Just real food, picked at its peak and carried straight to your table.
