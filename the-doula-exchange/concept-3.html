Money & Payments
Currency Reference
CountryCurrencyCodeTips
🇵🇹 Portugal Euro (€) EUR
Widely accepted. ATMs available. Cards accepted almost everywhere in Lisbon.
🇲🇦 Morocco Dirham (MAD) MAD
Cash preferred in souks and medina. Exchange at airport or banks. Avoid street exchanges.
🇳🇱 Netherlands Euro (€) EUR
Cards widely accepted. Contactless preferred. Some small vendors cash only.
🇧🇪 Belgium Euro (€) EUR
Same Euro zone as Portugal and Netherlands. Keep some cash for markets.
