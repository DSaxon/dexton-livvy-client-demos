Portal Preview

Doula Onboarding Portal

A private admin portal that guides your team through every step of onboarding a new doula, from initial application to final welcome. No Google Forms, no external tools, everything in one place.

📋

This is a functional prototype. The portal is fully operational with a live database, email notifications, and all 12 onboarding steps. Screenshots below show the real application running with sample doula data.

01 — Access
Secure Admin Login

Only authorized team members can access the portal. Admins sign in with their email and password. Session-based authentication keeps everything private and secure.

Login screen — branded to match The Doula Exchange website
02 — Overview
Command Dashboard

The dashboard gives admins an at-a-glance view of where every doula stands in the onboarding pipeline, which steps are creating bottlenecks, and recent activity across the team.

Dashboard — top-level stats, bottleneck chart, and recent doulas table
Scrolled view — stat cards and recent doulas with current onboarding step
03 — Management
Doulas Directory

A searchable, filterable list of all doulas in the system. Admins can search by name or email, filter by onboarding status, and click any row to open the full detail view.

Doulas tab — search bar, status filter, and full directory table
04 — Intake
Adding a New Doula

When a new doula is ready to onboard, admins click "Add Doula" to capture the essential information. The portal immediately creates their profile and initializes all 12 onboarding steps as pending.

Add Doula form — first name, last name, email, phone, address, training organization
05 — The 12-Step Workflow
Complete Onboarding Checklist

Every doula goes through the same structured 12-step process. Admins work through each step inside the portal, no Google Forms or external links required. Steps include forms, document uploads, an interview tracker, profile building, and the final welcome email.
