Our Story

Fresh out of the oven, since day one.

The beauty of baking lies in the cracking of the eggs, the melting of the butter, the blending of the cinnamons, nutmegs and spices — and the aroma that fills the air as a fresh cake emerges.

DC Sweet Potato Cake began with one recipe and grew into the region's only dedicated sweet potato bakery. We bake with orange, white and purple sweet potatoes, and our cakes are now on the shelves at grocers and coffee shops across Maryland, DC and Virginia.

Today we're building our bakery café in the Gateway Arts District in Hyattsville, a place to grab a slice, a sandwich, and a moment.

— April Richardson, Founder
