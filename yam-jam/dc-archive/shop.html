YAM JAM by Mrs. Miller

Sweet Citrus Glaze · 12 oz

YAM JAM is a sweet citrus glaze created from a Miller family recipe that dates back to the mid-1900s in Washington, D.C.

Originally served with sweet potatoes, the sauce has become a family favorite for a variety of foods. Enjoy it with chicken, ham, seafood, roasted vegetables, biscuits, pancakes and desserts.

Whether you're preparing a familiar family recipe or trying something new, YAM JAM is an easy addition to your kitchen.
