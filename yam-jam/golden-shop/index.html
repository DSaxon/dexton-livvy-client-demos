Meet YAM JAM

A Sweet Citrus Glaze for Every Table

Glaze your chicken, drizzle it over seafood, add something special to roasted vegetables, or bring a sweet citrus finish to breakfast and dessert. YAM JAM is a versatile family recipe that has been enjoyed at the Miller family table for generations.

First created in Mrs. Louise B. Miller's Washington, D.C. kitchen in the mid-1900s, YAM JAM became a family favorite, particularly on the sweet potatoes served at family gatherings. Her recipe has been passed down through generations, and now the Miller family is sharing it with yours.

From Mrs. Miller's Kitchen

For the Miller family, YAM JAM was simply part of the food they grew up enjoying. Its sweet citrus flavor was especially memorable on sweet potatoes, but the family soon discovered that it was just as delicious on other dishes.

Today, YAM JAM is available in a 12-ounce jar, ready to become part of the meals and traditions you create at home.
