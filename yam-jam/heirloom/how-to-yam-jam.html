How to Yam Jam

One Sweet Citrus Glaze, So Many Ways to Enjoy It

YAM JAM first became a family favorite as a glaze for sweet potatoes, but its sweet citrus flavor works with a surprising variety of foods. Whether you're preparing a holiday dinner, cooking on a weeknight or putting together something for breakfast, there are plenty of opportunities to bring YAM JAM to the table.

The Original

Sweet Potatoes

The original family favorite. Pour YAM JAM over warm sweet potatoes for the sweet citrus flavor that has been part of Miller family meals since the mid-1900s.

Ham & Roasts

Brush YAM JAM over ham or your favorite roast as it cooks, or add it toward the end for a glossy finish and a little citrus sweetness.

Chicken

Use YAM JAM as a glaze, incorporate it into a marinade, or brush it over cooked chicken just before serving. Its sweetness works especially well with roasted or grilled dishes.

Seafood

Try YAM JAM with salmon, shrimp or your favorite fish. Its citrus flavor makes a wonderful complement to seafood, whether brushed on during cooking or added as a finishing glaze.

Vegetables

Roasted carrots, Brussels sprouts, squash and other vegetables are delicious with a little YAM JAM. Add it toward the end of roasting or drizzle it over vegetables before serving.

Biscuits & Breakfast

Warm biscuits, pancakes, French toast and other breakfast favorites are an easy way to enjoy YAM JAM. Try a little in place of your usual syrup or fruit topping.

Desserts

Drizzle YAM JAM over pound cake, ice cream or a favorite dessert when you want to add a sweet citrus finish. It also pairs beautifully with simple desserts that allow its flavor to stand out.
