The Cost of Doing Nothing

Unmanaged Conflict Is Expensive

Most organizations treat conflict as an HR problem. They put up with it, shuffle people around, or hope it resolves itself. It doesn't.

Unresolved conflict bleeds into your bottom line through turnover, absenteeism, lost productivity, and the talent you never managed to keep. The cost of a 90-minute mediation session is nothing compared to replacing a key employee.

Lost Annually to Workplace Conflict

US businesses lose hundreds of billions every year to unresolved conflicts affecting productivity and output.

Our Framework

Five Steps.
One Resolution.

Built on the AMA conflict resolution framework and refined through hundreds of real engagements. No filler, no fluff — just what works.

STEP_01

Create a Safe Space

Neutral setting. Clear ground rules. The foundation everything else is built on.

STEP_02

Listen Actively

No interruptions. No assumptions. Full presence from every party in the room.

STEP_03

Find the Real Issue

The stated problem is never the whole problem. We go deeper to find what's actually driving it.

STEP_04

Build Solutions

Co-created options that work for everyone — not imposed compromises that collapse in a week.

STEP_05

Commit and Follow Up

Written agreements. Clear owners. A scheduled check-in. Because accountability is what makes it real.
