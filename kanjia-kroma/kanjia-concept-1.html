Our Framework

The Five-Step Resolution Process

A proven, structured approach built on the AMA framework — adapted for the complexity of real organizations and real people.

01

Create a Safe Space

Before anything else, all parties need to feel safe speaking openly. We establish ground rules, choose neutral settings, and ensure every voice enters the room on equal footing.

02

Listen Actively and Without Judgment

Most conflicts escalate because people feel unheard. We slow the conversation down, ensure each party is truly listened to, and surface what's actually being said beneath the surface.

03

Identify the Underlying Issue

The stated problem is rarely the real problem. We peel back the layers to find the root cause — separating people from positions and interests from demands.

04

Develop Mutually Beneficial Solutions

With the real issue visible, we co-create solutions that work for everyone — not forced compromises, but integrative agreements that expand what's possible.

05

Agree on a Plan and Follow Up

A conversation without accountability is just a wish. We formalize agreements in writing, assign owners, and schedule follow-up to make sure resolution sticks.
