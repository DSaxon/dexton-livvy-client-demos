Our Process

Five Steps to Real Resolution

Conflict resolution is not about picking sides. It's about uncovering what's underneath, and building a bridge to what's next.

1

Create a Safe Space

Neutral setting, clear ground rules, and the assurance that every voice matters.

2

Listen Actively

Full presence, no interruptions, and genuine curiosity about each person's experience.

3

Identify the Real Issue

Peel back the surface disagreement to find the root cause driving the conflict.

4

Develop Solutions Together

Co-create options that serve everyone's core needs — not forced compromise.

5

Agree and Follow Up

A written plan with clear owners, timelines, and a scheduled check-in to make it stick.
